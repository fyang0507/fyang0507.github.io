(I)
Sometimes time takes on a grain
as when nothing happens
and fingertips tremble against the artery's intent
as over the open plain
the starry sky and wheatfield may each grow
music on separate channels
inside some hybrid dream
phosphor-fire like water  warm snow like salt


(II)
Things made by God
being and nothingness  light and dark  one person and another
Things made by man
shackles  accidents  Fermat's little theorem
Before impact, you see
your life will often flash before your eyes
but when I collided with you, it didn't
nothing did
why?


(III)
Out of white noise accumulates
a butcher-block cloud—an omen of a turning sky
When the storm is about to break
electronic transition: impossible
one more jump back into the prelude
and the gesture, after countless attempts to clench, can only keep saying
even so  even so…
even so  one must take a dialectical view…


(IV)
When shadow swallows the moon
I unravel the secret of passing each other by
Contestants on dating shows, you see
aren't they gifted at factoring?
So this is a math problem
An encounter whose inner product is zero is no encounter
—yet coprime, too, and—
born prime, isn't that also
a kind of romance?
The tide, having grasped this, for some reason sud—
went dim

(Noise)
A tight weave of drumbeats
the movement turns

Modulate upward
localized heat beginning in the spinal cord
gradually becomes
a vibration traversing every inch of shed skin
watching and waiting
with just one slit left open
it can airdrop into the abyss of consciousness
self is current
current is channel


(V)
Point sources of light
drag their tails through the river channel
a duality born when the medium changes
these relatively stationary
comets
cannot carry any
biological wish


(VI)
Disguised as liquid
descending deeper underground
an analysis of probability
a train running upstream through the dark
At the end of the dream  aging is
a dry evolution
while entropy dissolves every
hypothesis of “I could have”
too immense, isn't it—
this monotonically increasing sin


(VII)

Secret Technique  Pre-established Harmony
repeat for the 3,957th time
step left-foot first into the absolute realm of the emergency stairwell
Because of this caution
today's room, before I have even opened the lock,
still strains to maintain
a form adapted to darkness


(VIII)
Vows are shackles
no wonder I travel light
There is water at the right temperature to dissolve voicing and throat
How do you describe a tomorrow that never disappears?
Safe, perhaps
Beyond that
it deserves no word with feeling in it

（一）
时间有时会成为一种颗粒感的东西
正如无事发生时
指尖违背动脉的意图而颤抖
正如旷野上
星空和麦田也许各自长出
不同声道的音乐
在某种杂交的梦境中
磷火似水 温雪如盐


（二）
神造的事物
有与无 明与暗 人与人
人造的事物
枷锁 事故 费马小定理
相撞之前 你看
常常是出现走马灯的
我撞上你时却没有
什么都没有
为什么呢？


（三）
从白噪音处累积的
砧板云 这是变天的前兆
山雨欲来之时
跃迁不能
再一次跳回前奏
而无数次尝试握紧的手势只是不断说出
即使这样 即使这样…
即使这样 也要以辩证的眼光…


（四）
阴影吞没月亮的时刻
我解开了关于擦肩而过的秘密
相亲节目里的嘉宾 你看
不是擅长因式分解吗
所以这是个数学问题
内积为零的邂逅就不是邂逅
——但互质也、
生为素数不也
是一种浪漫啊。
明白了这一点的潮汐不知为什么突
就黯淡下来

（杂讯）
以绵密的鼓点
转换乐章

升调
从脊髓开始的局部发热
渐渐转变为
遍历每寸肤屑的震感
伺机等待
仅仅漏开一道口子
就可以向意识的深渊空降
自我即水流
水流即通道


（五）
点状光源
在河道中拖尾
因介质更改而出现的二象性
这些相对静止的
彗星
无法携带任何
生物性的愿望


（六）
以液体的伪装
降入更深的地底
关于概率的解析
黑暗中溯流而上的列车
在梦的尽头 衰老是
一种干燥的进化
而熵溶解掉每一个
「我本可以」的假设
过于庞大了不是么
这单增的罪孽


（七）

秘技 预定调和
重复第三千九百五十七次
用左脚迈入安全梯的绝对领域
因为这份谨慎
今天的房间在我还未开启门锁之前
仍然全力保持适应
黑暗的模样


（八）
誓言即枷锁
难怪我一身轻松
有种水温足以消融发声和咽喉
要怎样形容永不消逝的明天呢？
安全吧
除此之外
它实在配不上任何带有色彩的语汇
