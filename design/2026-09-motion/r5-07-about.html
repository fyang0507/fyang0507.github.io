BOARD 07 · ROUND 5

Flip on the card, put back on the sleeve 在卡上翻，往套里放

Round 4 took the card out of the side, but in the hand it made one axis do two jobs: a drag to the right turned the card over, a drag to the left put it back. So direction decided the outcome, and a natural flip toward the sleeve put the card away. It also lay the sleeve on its side, so the card had to turn a quarter to stand up, which Fred found unnecessary.

FRED, ON ROUND 4

“The region that ‘flip the card’ and ‘put the card back to sleeve’ is somewhat unintuitive and hard to figure out. When I want to flip the card, the action put it back to sleeve. Plus the sleeve doesn't have to be in horizontal, you can still make it vertical and take the card out without rotation from the side.”

The sleeve stands upright with its mouth on its long right edge. The card slides out sideways, catches on the lip, pops free and is lifted into your hand beside the sleeve, with no turn. The landscape build and its toggle are gone. The card's left margin (the name's first stroke, the row labels) is the last strip under the lip.

Flip and put back no longer share a direction. Flip lives on the card: tap it, or flick it either way and it turns over that way. Putting it back is a place, not a direction: carry the card onto the sleeve. As you bring it near, the sleeve answers: the lip bows toward it, a coral 「 」 marks the mouth and the card dips toward the desk. Once its centre is over the sleeve, the sleeve takes it: the card docks half into the mouth at desk size (push further and it goes deeper; pull away and it comes back out into your hand). Let go while it is docked and it slides the rest of the way in. Anything else settles back into your hand on the face it was showing, so a slow drag never flips. Tapping the sleeve, its printed 放回 · put back, or pressing Esc also puts the card back. A pencil hand-note beside the card teaches both until you have done each once in the session.

the release rule · 松手规则 — measured over the last 80 ms before you let go: ① the card's centre is over the sleeve (the sleeve and 60px out from its mouth) and it has arrived, slower than 900 px/s, or having lingered there (slowly) for 100 ms → put back. A sleeve can't catch a card flung through it; ② otherwise, moving sideways at ≥ 600 px/s (sideways ≥ 1.2× vertical) after ≤ 170px of travel, or at ≥ 1400 px/s at any length → a flick: it flips toward the flick, either way; ③ anything else → it settles back in your hand, same face. A tap (< 6px, < 450 ms) flips.

RECOMMENDATION

Ship this as the About card. The pull is round 4's short-axis pull, with no rotation, as Fred asked. The hand now separates its two acts by kind rather than by direction. Flip is something you do to the card (a tap, or a flick either way). Putting it back is somewhere you take the card (onto the sleeve), and the sleeve answers before you let go, so the target is visible before you commit. A drag that is neither settles back without doing anything. Nothing irreversible happens by accident, and a flick toward the sleeve still flips, because a flick never travels as far as the sleeve.

Watch: on a phone the card fills the width and the sleeve sits above it, so "onto the sleeve" is an upward move. A vertical swipe that starts on the card belongs to the page and scrolls, so a touch drag to the sleeve has to start sideways before it curves up. Tapping the sleeve, or its printed 放回, is the easy way there, and the hand-note says so. The pull is a card width long (0.9 of its desk width, 233px at 1440×900), shorter than round 4's lengthwise pull. That is the cost of not turning the card.

执行模式：精修（第五轮 · 卡套竖放；翻面与放回分开） 表达锚点：竖放的牛皮纸卡套，右侧长边开口；卡从侧面抽出，拿在手里；翻面是对卡做的动作，放回是把卡带到一个地方。 动作时钟：物理时钟 —— 往右抽（先粘住、再打滑，摩擦随套里剩下的宽度递减，握卡套的手往反方向让一点）、卡在卡套口、松脱（卡套弹回并扭一下）、拿起到卡套旁边；手里：跟着手走、按速度前倾；轻拨 → 顺着拨的方向翻面（一次小回弹）；靠近卡套 → 卡往桌面沉一点、卡套口张开、卡套口向卡弯；在卡套上松手 → 被接住、放进卡套口、带摩擦推进去；别处松手 → 回到手里，同一面；手的时钟 —— 两帧冲击线、小鸟定格、雷达逐笔画出、清单钢笔勾。 放回：拖近卡套 → 卡套口向卡弯、珊瑚色「 」标出卡套口、卡往桌面沉；卡心进入卡套区 → 卡套“接住”它，桌面大小、半插进卡套口（再推更深，拉开就回到手里）；此时松手 → 顺势推进去。 映射（松手规则）：卡心在卡套区内，且是“到了”（松手速度 < 900 px/s，或在区内慢速停留 ≥ 100 ms）→ 放回；否则，横向速度 ≥ 600 px/s、横向 ≥ 1.2 倍竖向、位移 ≤ 170 px（或横向速度 ≥ 1400 px/s，不论位移）→ 顺着方向轻拨翻面；否则 → 回到手里，同一面。点击（位移 < 6 px、< 450 ms）→ 翻面。点卡套、卡套上的“放回”、Esc → 放回。 提示：卡第一次拿到手里时，旁边一段铅笔手写批注说明两件事；各做过一次后本次会话不再出现。 笔：悬停下划线与焦点「 」、卡套口的“这里”「 」为珊瑚色（短暂）；提示箭头为 r3-02 箭头字形，珊瑚色，第一次抽出后本次会话不再出现。 色彩计划：牛皮纸为结构色；静止时珊瑚色只在日面手写批注 / 夜面雷达（以及抽出前的一支提示箭头）；无高光、无流光、无光晕，接触阴影用网点。
