BOARD 05 · ROUND 4

The flower answers 小红花会回应

Fred shipped F1, the 小红花, with an ink pin, and asked for more of the press he liked: in round 3 it happened once, when the board first came into view, and never again — so the one motion that says “this is the pick” was spent before most people had looked. Now the press is the flower's behaviour. It answers attention (hover, keyboard focus), coming back to it, and the pin going back in; each time with a cause, never on a loop.

1 · once per session

First view 初见

60% of the word on screen, then a third of a second: it arrives in the fingers and is pressed on. Before that the card has no flower.

2 · fine pointer

Hover 指到

The pointer arrives on the card: it pops up and is pressed back. Once per entry — resting, moving inside the card or dragging never repeat it.

3 · keyboard

Focus 聚焦

Tab arrives on the card from outside: the same pop, with coral 「 」 round the title. Tabbing on to its link doesn't repeat it.

4 · drag, swipe, scroll

Return 回来

The board or the page took the card away (under 5% visible) and brought it back: pressed once the board has come to rest, not while it swings.

5 · the pin

Re-pin 钉回去

The card lands, the flower jolts up, and it is pressed down on the pin's impact frame — one thumb, one thump, both things.

SHIP

Ship the board as it stands: A + C in one physics loop, the 小红花 on the lead card, ink pin, and the press as the flower's answer to attention. The press now does what Fred wanted from it: it makes the highlight a behaviour you meet, not a sticker you might miss. It stays restrained because it only ever answers you — your pointer, your Tab, you coming back, your thumb on the pin — once per cause, with a cooldown, and never on its own.

For the port: take r4-05-unpin.js (held frames fixed), r4-05-sticker.js + r4-05-cues.js for the flower, and r4-05-pen.js only as a stand-in — the production pen (L1) should come from board 02's round-4 code. Drop board 02's “start here” arrow on Building, since the flower is already the point there.
