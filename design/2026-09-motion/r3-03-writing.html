The index 索引

The index stands where the live preview card was. It is a closed book, cover toward you, with a title slip (题签) reading 类别 · tags. Six paper tabs come out of its fore-edge: all · 全部, then each category by size, each with its English and Chinese name and a count. Each tab has a small glued lip where it enters the pages and a pencil line along the page beneath it. The chosen tab springs further out on the physics clock, with one small overshoot. A tab with nothing in the chosen years sinks back under the cover, but you can still click it.

Directly under the tabs, the year ledger is a ruled page with one line per calendar year, 2020 included, and each year’s 正 tally. Click a year to choose it, or drag down the ledger to choose a span. The books reflow while you drag, and a paper flag beside the pointer names the span. Drag from either end of a span to resize it. Hovering any tab or year lifts its books a finger’s width off the plank.

The bookcase 书架

This is r2-04 A’s camera and paper boxes. At the new size the eye sits above the top plank, so both rows show their page-block tops, the lower row more. Each book keeps a home plank, the one it stands on when all 27 are out. A filter never moves a book to another plank: the books left on each plank close up toward its start. The upper plank holds 2026–2022 and the lower one 2021–2015, and each plank’s front lip carries a small printed shelf label saying so (“2026 — 2022”), in pencil ink. Choosing 2015–2019 empties the upper plank; its label dims a step, so the empty plank reads as “none from these years”, not a broken layout. A pulled book is held in front of the other row, so the gap it left stays in view: from the lower plank it rises in front of the upper row, and from the upper plank it comes down in front of the lower one.

Pen and colour 笔与颜色

Board 02’s tiers: hover = a level underline in the highlighter’s thin edge; chosen = the band, drawn in one return stroke; no tick; keyboard focus = ink 「 」. The tallies are ink and are rewritten stroke by stroke when the tag changes. The pen under a held book’s obi title is the same thin edge. Coral stays on 写.

When the two disagree 两者冲突时

The dimension you touch wins; the other one gives way.你碰哪一个，哪一个说了算；另一个让开。

The rule is unchanged from round 2, and the filter controller is r2-03-filter.js loaded as is. The value that gave way stays in the readout, struck through in pencil. 2020 is empty for every tag, so choosing it shows an empty bookcase with a pencilled note.

On a phone 手机上

There is no room for a side index, so the same index becomes one strip above the shelf: the readout, then the tabs standing on their page block (swipe them), then the ledger turned on its side as twelve year columns (drag across them). Both filters sit together, and the shelf directly below is r2-04 A’s swipe strip: tap to pull a book out, tap again to open it.

RECOMMENDATION

Ship A. It makes the two changes Fred asked for and keeps what he accepted. Both filters sit in one index on the side, where a closed book carries its tabs. The shelf is bigger and taller and spans the full content width. The pieces he liked are unchanged: the 正 tallies and their recount, the readout, the reflow physics, the pull with its obi, and the open.

How r2-04 A and r2-03’s reflow fit together. Neither had to give anything up, but three things changed to make them coexist. 1. The reflow engine now reads the bookcase’s slot positions instead of DOM layout boxes. Its offsets are added to the pull pose before the single 3D transform is written, so one book can be hooked and reflowing at the same moment. A leaving book fades face by face, because opacity on the box itself would flatten its 3D. 2. A book never changes planks. My first build carried books across planks when one emptied, and nine books flying up at once read as a swarm, not a hand, so every book now keeps its home plank. 3. A pulled book rises or drops only once it is clear of the plank in front of it, and r2-04’s “the plank is solid” bounce only applies while the book is still over its plank. Starting a filter puts a held book back first.

Watch: under the real site header at 1440×900 (full page ↗), the top plank and the whole index are above the fold and the lower plank ends about 150 px below it. That is the cost of taller books. On a phone the index strip comes before the shelf, so the shelf starts near the fold. Gallery can use the same index component with its own nouns (frames, 张, the clothesline); r2-03 already showed that the controller carries over, and it is not rebuilt here.
