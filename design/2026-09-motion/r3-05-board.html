The corkboard's pick 主卡上的小红花

Fred liked W4 — a small sticker someone stuck on at the end of “Fred Agent” — but an asterisk says “see the sidenote”, not “this is the one”. It was worse than the glyph: the sticker sat in superscript position and the label's dot became a second asterisk, so the pair read as a footnote and its mark. The lead card needs a mark people already give to the pick, in the same place, at the same size.

The pin: ink 图钉保持墨色

A coral sticker and a coral pin on the same card are two spends, not one. The rule counts what the eye has to reconcile, and here they are two small coral events ~145px apart on desktop and ~195px on a phone, with the wordmark between them — the glance goes pin → mark → pin. They also mean different things: the mark is on the paper and travels with it when you take the card down; the pin is the board's hardware, shared by every card in six muted colours, so a coral one reads as “a red pin”, not “the pick”. Keep the pin ink. W5's idea only works if it is the only coral on the card — i.e. instead of a mark, which is round 2's W5 and the weakest of its five.

RECOMMENDATION

F1, the little red flower, with an ink pin. It keeps everything Fred liked about W4 — a small thing a person stuck on, in the same place at the same size — and swaps the meaning from “see the note” to “this one was picked”. A 小红花 is given, not earned by default: a teacher chose it for the best work of the day. Stuck on your own project by your own hand, it is also a dry self-deprecating joke — a 小红花 you gave yourself — which keeps it 萌 rather than 媚. It needs no text, so no language is left out, and it survives the phone size because it is only five round petals and a centre.

The real alternative is F3, the 荐 seal, if Fred wants “recommended” said outright and prefers a press to a sticker: it is the most precise meaning and the crispest motion, but it adds a Chinese glyph to the card (paired by the English label, and it needs 荐 in the font subset) and sits closest to the red-square emoji. The star is the safe universal and therefore generic; the rosette is the busiest at 27px and says “first prize”, which is competition rather than praise.
