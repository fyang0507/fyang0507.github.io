RECOMMENDATION

Ship A at 7:12. It is exactly r2 with the shape Fred asked for. The card reads as something you'd keep in a book, not a badge on a lanyard. The sleeve is now a tall envelope that fits the card, not a square pouch. The pull is longer because the card is longer, and since the whole gesture now fits one screen, the catch and the lift happen where you can see them.

The proportion switch under the stage runs the same rig at 5:8, an index card and close to an ID card. It is the only alternative worth showing, and it is close. At the same height it is 6% wider on desktop, and on a phone, where the width is fixed, it comes out 7% smaller, type included. It drifts back toward the badge Fred objected to. Keep 7:12.

Watch: in the hand the card is sized to the screen's height (up to 740px), so a short laptop gets a smaller card, but every face is laid out in card units, so it is the same card and never a different layout. At 1440×900 it is 390×668: within 12px of r2's 680 in height, and a quarter narrower. On a 1080p screen it grows to 420×720. On screens shorter than about 850px it stops shrinking at its reference size (the phone's type sizes), and there the page scrolls a little instead. The narrowing is what fixes "fat". Making it taller than the screen would bring the fold problem back. The re-sleeve drag lowers the card toward the mouth before you push it in. Because the lifted card now sits a little higher than where it left the desk, the first 100px of an upward drag dip it slightly as it shrinks. That is physically right (you set it down, then slide it in), but it is the one place the new geometry shows.

执行模式：精修（第三轮 · 只改比例，不改交互） 表达锚点：标本卡是一张可以夹在书里的卡片，不是挂在胸前的胸牌：七比十二的竖长比例，卡套是贴身的细长纸袋。 动作时钟：物理时钟 —— 抽出（先粘住、再打滑；摩擦随卡在套里的长度递减，最后三分之一几乎不拖）、卡在卡套口（卡套口下垂、卡套被带着走）、松脱（卡套弹回并扭一下）、拿起到手里（放大、向你抬起一点、更长的卡前倾更多）、倾斜（横向多、纵向少）、甩动翻面（窄卡转动惯量小，翻得略快）、半色调接触阴影、推回卡套；手的时钟 —— 两帧冲击线、小鸟的定格姿势、雷达逐笔画出、卡套清单上的钢笔勾。 映射：手指往下拖 → 卡片滑出，套里剩得越多越慢；卡片上沿碰到卡套口 → 卡住、卡套口被拉弯；拉力超过摩擦 → 卡片弹出；卡片离开桌面 → 放大到一屏之内、卡套抬头的印字仍露在上方；第一次拿到某一面 → 清单打勾；抵达夜面 → 见证者开始记录；往上推 → 放回卡套口，再推进去。 比例：卡 7:12（竖长）；卡套约 1:1.5（第二轮 1:1.05）；所有卡面尺寸以卡高的 1/576 为单位，手机与桌面是同一张卡的两个尺寸。 线条计划：卡套边缘、缝线、纤维、冲击线、提示箭头（改为短而近直）都是单笔、钝头、铅笔 / 墨色；悬停只用笔的下划线（改用荧光笔细边的赭色，不再用珊瑚色）。 色彩计划：牛皮纸为结构色；日面珊瑚色只留给手写批注，夜面珊瑚色只留给雷达多边形；无高光、无流光、无光晕，接触阴影用网点。
