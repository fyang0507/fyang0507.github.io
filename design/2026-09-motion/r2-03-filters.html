Two controls, one grammar 两个控件，一套语法

Both dimensions sit in the same left column with the same small bilingual label, 类别 · tags above the shelf and 年份 · years below it, so the books sit between their two filters. Each has an explicit “all” (the first divider; all · 全部 beside the ruler), each shows its counts (numbers on the tabs, 正 tallies on the ruler), and each dims what the other has emptied without disabling it.

The ruler 年份尺

Every year from 2026 to 2015 gets an equal cell, including 2020, which has none: a ruler that skips a year misrepresents the gaps. Click once for a year and again for every year. Drag across the ruler and the coral brackets hop from cell to cell on a spring while a paper flag names the span (“2015–2019 · 11 篇”). The books tip out and drop in while you drag (at most one reflow per 90ms). Drag from a bracketed end to resize, or use shift-click and shift+←/→. Hover a year or a tab and its books rise a finger’s width off the plank, so the controls point at the books.

The tallies are 正-counting: one stroke per essay, five strokes make 正, the way Chinese classrooms count votes. Three strokes read as three to anyone. When the tag changes the ruler recounts by hand: extra strokes lift off, and missing ones are written one at a time, left to right.

When the two disagree 两者冲突时

The dimension you touch wins; the other one gives way.你碰哪一个，哪一个说了算；另一个让开。

Choose commentary (sunk, since it has none in 2015–2019) and the years release to every year. The readout keeps 2015–2019 on the line with a pencil strike until your next move. Click a dim year and the tag releases the same way. A span that is empty for every tag (2020) stays honestly empty.

On a phone 手机上

The dividers become a swipe strip that scrolls the chosen one into view. The ruler takes the full width with ’26-style labels and cells about 30×59px. touch-action: pan-y lets a horizontal thumb-drag select a span while a vertical one still scrolls the page, and the flag rides above the thumb, not under it. The readout wraps to two lines: what is chosen, then how many.

It generalises 可以通用

The filter only asks its collection for five things: cats, years, items, noun, apply(). The Gallery rack answers with 107 real frames and nine dividers (cityscape 28 · landscape 20 · people 19 …), and the dividers now rise from behind the first clothesline instead of the shelf edge. The ruler runs 2025 → 2015. Photo counts are big enough that the 正 tallies become a real histogram: 2024’s 29 frames are five full 正 plus four strokes.

Same engine, hanging physics 同一引擎，悬挂物理

The reflow engine gained one option, hang. A print that leaves loses one peg, swings about the other and drops. One that arrives is hung on the line and swings from its peg as it lands. As on the live page, the rack shows three lines and a “+68 more on the line · 还有 68 张” tag for the rest, while the readout always states the full count.

What differs 区别

Identical tabs, ruler, tallies, readout and yield rule. Only the year model changes: years are a set, not a span. Each tap circles or uncircles one year in pen, so any combination works (2015, 2019, 2024), and the readout collapses neighbouring years (“2015, 2018–2019”).

Gained: non-adjacent years, for “my 2015 and 2019 trips”. Nothing to learn beyond tapping, and no drag gesture that could fight page scroll on a phone. Lost: a decade takes ten taps instead of one drag, the brackets’ “window on time” reading goes away, and the readout can grow long.

The round-1 loops, promoted onto the explicit ruler. Simpler to operate, weaker at the thing a timeline is for.
cost slightly less than A (no drag, no brackets) · a11y ruler = multiselectable listbox, Enter/Space toggles, Esc clears · support everywhere
RECOMMENDATION

A · span by drag. Years are ordered, so the natural question is “from when to when”. A answers it in one gesture, reflows live while you drag, and the readout reads back as “游记 travel log · 2015–2019 · 11 essays”. Clicking a single year is still one tap, so A loses nothing to B for the common case.

Keep from B the loop vocabulary for anything that is a set rather than a span. Watch two things when this ships. First, the coral budget: the filter spends coral only while a dimension is narrowed, so at rest it adds none to the page. Second, the phone ruler cells are about 30×59px, which clears WCAG 2.2’s 24px minimum but is narrower than 44. Dragging makes precise aim unnecessary, and the flag confirms the year above the thumb.
