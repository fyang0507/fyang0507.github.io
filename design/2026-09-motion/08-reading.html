Reading, with a pencil in hand 阅读

Reading is where the words live, and it has the least authored detail. On posts with a cover the sticky nav is transparent, so the essay scrolls straight through “← 全部文章” and the EN/◐ buttons. Citations are set in handwriting: dates and titles in Caveat and MuyaoPleased are hard to read, and they spend the rationed hand voice on the least hand-like content on the page. A ten-section essay gives no sense of place beyond a 2px coral bar, because its “1.0 / 2.0” markers are bold text with no role. The cover wash is static and murky, and the essay ends on a hairline and a card.

APencil margin 铅笔页边scroll-linked stroke · pen loop on the current section · hand’s clock for marks, physics clock for the peek slip
A graphite line in the empty left margin draws as you read. Each section is a tick labelled in utility mono; hover or focus one to preview its first line, click to go there. The section you are in gets the pen loop, and the in-body markers become real headings with anchors. On a phone it collapses to a pencil hairline under the nav and a “3.0 / 10” counter.
cost one SVG, one scroll handler, no layout thrash (section offsets are cached on layout) · a11y real <nav> of anchors, ↑/↓ between ticks, preview on focus · reduced motion the line steps per section, with no continuous drawing · replaces the 2px coral bar
BFootnotes that point 会指的脚注hover / focus → pen connector + loop (hand’s clock) · touch → paper slip on a spring (physics clock)
Margin notes are reset in small upright serif, with sources in ink and dates in utility mono. The full reference replaces the truncated “Flas…”. Hovering or focusing a reference number draws a dotted pencil leader from it through the leading, never across glyphs, then one stroke down the gutter that loops the note’s number. Hovering the note draws the line back. On a phone, tapping a number slides the note up from the bottom as a paper slip, which you swipe down or tap away.
cost the connector is one path built on demand, so nothing runs while idle · a11y refs stay real links to the reference list, focus draws the same line, Esc closes the slip and focus returns to the ref · hand goes back to asides only · dark press ◐ in the demo’s nav

Select any passage and a flat peach marker band is laid behind those lines, with hand-cut ends and never over the ink. A small tag offers 引用 quote, which copies the passage with a deep link that scrolls a friend to it, or 复制 copy. Marks persist for this essay; click one to erase it.

DHero settles 题图落定animation-timeline: view() · halftone dissolve · the nav as a window onto the wash
At rest the cover is crisper than today. As you read, it drifts a little slower than the page, and paper eats it from below as halftone dots rather than a gradient. The nav is always opaque, but it paints the exact slice of wash behind it, so at the top it looks transparent while text can never pass through it. The paper and hairline arrive when the wash is gone, and the title tucks into the nav as the heading leaves. At the end, the previous essay’s cover develops out of the same dots.

RECOMMENDATION

D + B, built as one change. D fixes the overlap bug structurally rather than cosmetically. The nav never becomes transparent to content; it only paints what is behind it. D also turns the murky static wash into an opening that settles and an ending that develops. B returns handwriting to asides and gives every citation a legible home, and its pointing line is the pen doing its one job: connecting a mark to the note it refers to.

Add A’s section rail as a second step once D and B ship. It is the right answer to “where am I in ten sections”, but it adds persistent chrome to the margin, so it should arrive after the page is quiet and correct. The mocks already show the 2px progress bar in graphite rather than coral; retire it entirely when A lands. Park C: highlights are a product feature with storage, sync and mobile-callout questions, not a detail. Watch for three things. In D, the nav’s wash slice must share the page wash’s timeline, or a one-pixel seam appears. In B, the leader must stay dotted and in the leading; a solid rule there reads as an underline of the row above. Neither may add a second coral event to a view.
