RECOMMENDATION

Ship A with C folded in, and W2. The board is one material now: moving it swings the paper, taking a card down drafts its neighbours, pinning it back thumps the cork. Every motion has a hand behind it — yours, the board's edge, the pin — and at rest nothing moves. For the wordmark, the level rule keeps what Fred accepted in round 1 (flat ink, the pen saying “highlighted”) and removes exactly what he objected to: the bow and the lift.

Watch the tuning: the press ripple is the one register break on this page (~3° at the nearest card, under 1° at the far end), and the wake is kept smaller than it so the two never compete. If the ripple feels like a party trick after the tenth time, halve PRESS.ampDeg — don't remove it; it is what makes re-pinning feel like a pin going into cork. W3 (loop) is the runner-up if Fred wants the wordmark to feel more annotated; W5 only works if the other pins go ink.
