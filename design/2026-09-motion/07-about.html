The field-guide card — day form 架桥者 · BUILDER, night form 见证者 · WITNESS — is the site's most characterful object, and its interaction argues with the taste. Pointer tilt paints a glossy radial white glare across it, the FLIP button runs an endless shimmer sweep, and the flip itself is a button-triggered rotateY of a zero-thickness plane: a screen pretending to be a card. The night side's radar arrives already finished, and the contact row is five generic circles.

A thick mounted specimen card that you hold. Tilt now shows its greyboard edge instead of a glare, and a halftone contact shadow slides opposite the tilt and narrows as the card turns. Drag sideways and let go: the spring picks the nearest face from the angle and the release velocity, overshoots once, then settles. On arrival at night the witness takes notes: rings, spokes and the polygon each draw in one pen pass, and the six grades are stamped in on held beats. The bird on the corner only moves when the card moves under it.

The day form is a printed label stuck on the night card. Pull the dog-ear and the label folds back along a line that follows your finger; the flap shows the label's reverse, the print ghosting through the paper. Past about a tenth of the label it peels off and hangs from the card's side; press it to smooth it back on. It says something true — night is underneath — but makes the day form a cover rather than a face.

Two cards, one kraft sleeve. When the stage arrives the day card slides out of the sleeve on a spring and the sleeve gives a little the other way; flipping tucks it back and draws the night card out, and a pen tick on the sleeve's printed checklist moves to the form that is out. Tactile and collectible — but it is a swap, not a turn: the two forms stop being two sides of one person.

RECOMMENDATION

A, Hold the card. It keeps the object as one card with two sides, which is what the page is saying (“by day, I make things happen; by night, I keep them from disappearing”). It fixes the gloss by moving physicality from light to matter: glare goes, and edge, contact and weight come in. It gives the flip a cause your own hand owns, grab and throw, and it gives the night side its one dense event, the witness filling in the chart.

Keep tilt at 7° or less so the edge is a glimpse, not a feature. Retire the 6 s bird bump loop: the bird now only reacts when the card moves under it. The redesigned FLIP control (turn glyph + pen underline) and the text-first contact row carry over to any candidate. B's dog-ear is the strongest idea from the other two; it could return later as the WIP-style “there's more under here” cue, but not as the primary flip.

执行模式：多阶段（候选 → 等待 Fred 选择） 表达锚点：标本卡是一张有厚度的卡纸，两面是同一个人的昼与夜。把它拿在手里、甩一下、翻过来。 动作时钟：物理时钟 —— 卡片的倾斜、拖拽翻面、接触阴影、二维码纸条（弹簧，一次小回弹后停稳）；手的时钟 —— 雷达图逐笔画出、六个评级按拍子盖章（约 8 帧/秒，无缓动）、小鸟的姿势切换（定格两帧）。 映射：指针位置 → 卡片被按下的一侧后仰，灰板纸边露出来；拖拽与松手速度 → 甩动惯性，落到最近的一面；卡片抬起 → 网点阴影变远、变淡；卡片转动 → 阴影随投影宽度收窄；抵达夜面 → 见证者开始记录（环、辐、多边形各一笔，评级逐拍落点）；卡片在脚下动 → 小鸟惊起一拍。 线条计划：雷达环与辐线用铅笔色单笔，带一点稳定的抖动；多边形用珊瑚色单笔、保留锐角、收笔略过起点；悬停只用笔的下划线；联系方式图标单线、钝头。 色彩计划：去掉白色高光、流光、人物背后的珊瑚光晕；日面珊瑚色只留给手写批注，夜面珊瑚色只留给雷达多边形（特殊技能框改为铅笔虚线）；接触阴影用半色调网点，不用模糊。
