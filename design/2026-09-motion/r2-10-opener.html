    RECOMMENDATION

    C → D with name beat a, 一字一切. It fixes exactly what felt weird and keeps exactly what felt like WOW. The drum of slams stays (弗, then 雷 and 德 quicker, then FRED), but each character stands alone, full-bleed, and FRED lands as the payoff. The pairing is across the sequence, never inside a frame, so nobody reads “RE” as a translation of 雷. It audits at ≤ 2.5 flashes/s on both readings, against a limit of 3.

    c, 书脊 is the one to take if you want the OP’s first beat to feel designed rather than loud. It’s one held poster: a spine title on a shelf with the bird as the publisher’s colophon, rhyming with the Writing shelf. It’s also the calmest on flashes (≤ 1.5/s). b, 对撞 is the most kinetic, two languages colliding into one person, but its entry and smear frames are motion, not posters. Watch-outs for any of them: the gag honestly slows down on a cold connection (that’s the joke, and the cap is 6.5 s); and the OP breaks the colour rules on purpose, for 1.5 s, once.
