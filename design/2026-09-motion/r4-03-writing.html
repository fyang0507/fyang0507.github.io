Why (a) and not (b) 为什么加层，不压平

A flat book’s footprint is its spine length, and a stack can only be as tall as the gap under the plank above. A year’s stack is about 250 px wide however few books it holds, and at 60–120 essays a year holds 3–7. So laying years flat costs more plank than standing them up: one extra plank at 1440, and three at 1024. It also turns the older three-quarters of the archive on its side. A capped case cannot hide books, so it grows anyway, only faster. The stacks are built (switch to (b)): they take the reflow, a flat book slides out of its stack and stands up in your hand, and the titles read horizontally. They are simply the worse rule.

What makes (a) hold 加层为什么撑得住

Books fill the planks in reading order, from the top. Round 3 kept a home plank for every book. At 120 that would have left a year filter’s few books spread down nine planks. Now a filter gathers its books at the top of the case. A book that has to change planks fades out where it stands and drops into its new slot (no flying across the case). The case shortens once they settle. If you filter from deep down the case, the page comes back to its top. The eye travels with you: the camera moves down with the scroll, as r2-04’s phone strip does sideways, so the ninth plank is drawn at the same angle as the first. The plank labels name the years standing on each plank now (“2026 — 2025”, “2025 — 2023”, …).

The book comes to the hand 书跟着手

When a book comes out, its held pose is placed from where the pointer is: the book rises only so far that the pointer lands 74% down its cover, on the obi. Every face of the held book takes the pointer. Moving from the slot onto the book keeps it out, clicking at the same spot opens it, and leaving both puts it back. The empty slot still opens it during the slide-out. Verified with a real pointer path (hover the spine → wait → click at the same point → the page opens): 4/4 on both planks, 1/1 for a flat book in a stack, and 4/4 taps on the phone at 27 and 120. The trade: the held book now covers part of its own slot, so less of the gap shows. Round 3 also had a bug where a click could re-shelve the book being clicked (a stale keyboard blur); it is fixed.

Pen 笔

This uses board 02’s round-4 tier, r4-02-tier.js, loaded as is. Hover draws a coral line under a tab, a year or clear. When you choose, the band sweeps over that line and the coral cools into the band’s darker wheat edge. You can watch 2012 and 2011 still coral mid-drag while 2014 and 2013 are already wheat. Keyboard 「 」 is coral. The pen under a held book’s obi title is coral again (notice). Nothing chosen is coral at rest.

On a phone 手机上

The phone is still one swipe strip with the index strip above it, and the growth rule doesn’t apply. At 120 the strip is about 13 screen-widths long, so the index is the way in: the ruler holds 18 years, with dense year labels below 24 px a column. Tap a spine and the book comes up under your finger; tap it again to open it.

The bookcase stays one continuous, chronological case and simply grows. The index sticks beside it, so any shelf is one click from any filter, and a filter gathers its books at the top so results are never scattered. The pulled book stays under your pointer, so the click that opens it lands on the book.

RECOMMENDATION

Fred’s question: yes, with rule (a). The multi-shelf design holds as the writing grows, as long as the case adds a plank per 13–14 books and stays one chronological case. At 120 essays that is 9 planks and about 3,300 px of page at 1440, a bookcase you scroll down. It holds because the index is pinned beside it, a filter gathers its books at the top, and the eye travels with you. Capping the case and laying old years flat (b) doesn’t save room: it needs more planks at 60 and 120, at both 1440 and 1024, and it puts most of the archive on its side.

The hover-to-click gap is closed: the pulled book stays under the pointer (or finger), and clicking the book opens it. Watch: past ~200 essays the page at 1440 would pass ~5,500 px. The filters still gather results at the top, but browsing “all” becomes a long scroll, and that is the point to add a decade jump beside the ledger. The phone strip is 13 screens long at 120, so there the index is the way in. The pen comes from board 02’s in-progress r4-02-tier.js; if its API changes, this board follows it.
