BOARD 02

The pen: one state language 笔：统一的状态语言

Every interactive state on the site is a stock default: links and tabs swap to coral on hover, the Writing and Gallery filters are SaaS pills with a coral border for "on", focus is a browser outline, and the subpage nav labels are 8.5px mono at 62% opacity — unreadable. Meanwhile the site's own signature, the hand-drawn coral stroke under the name and under 狂狷, is never used interactively. If the thesis is hand-annotated humanity, the interface should be annotated by the same hand.

ACircle what you mean 圈出来Pen.loop · hand's clock
A loop is the most unmistakable thing a pen can do: circling where you are on the nav reads like someone marking a map. But it is a loud gesture, and a loop around a word in running text breaks the line gap.
cost two coral loops at rest (current + selected) · loops overlap neighbouring lines in prose · a11y ink focus loop clears 3:1 · support all evergreen
BUnderline & tick 划线与打勾underline · tick · scribble · 「 」
Four marks, four meanings: an underline means "this one?", a tick means "chosen", the scribble is the identity stroke meaning "you are here", and hanging corner brackets 「 」 mean "the keyboard is here". Every mark lives in the word's own line box or line gap, so it works in a paragraph as well as in the nav.
cost one coral mark at rest; hover spends it briefly · a11y focus is a distinct shape, not a colour · support all evergreen
CThe pointing hand 指一下Pen.arrow · brackets · hand's clock
The home page's annotation arrows, used as state: a small pen arrow swings in from above and points at what you are about to press. The most editorial and the least chrome — but an arrow needs clear air, and prose has none.
cost needs ~30px of free space above every target · arrows collide with the line above in prose · a11y hover vs focus differ only by value (pencil → ink) · support all evergreen
DHighlighter wash 荧光笔marker band · masked sweep · colour-led
The colour-led alternative: a flat wheat band with scissor-cut ends is swept in behind the words, left to right, and never over them. No coral in the chrome at all — but a band behind text is a background fill wearing a hand-made edge, and it can't show focus on its own.
cost reads as study notes; competes with the Reading hero washes · a11y wheat behind soft text drops contrast to ~4.0:1, so text goes to ink · support SVG mask, all evergreen
RULE 1

Wobble, not jitter 是手抖，不是乱抖

Hover each word a few times. Every pass leaves a faint ghost. The seeded mark lands on its own ghost every time — one hand, one circle. The re-rolled one draws a new circle each hover and builds a bundle: evidence of nobody.

seed

draw
200 + 1.1 × length ms, capped at 620 — a tick lands in ~⅕ s, a long loop in ~⅗ s. One pass, never two.
ease
cubic-bezier(.55,.1,.25,1) — quick start, decelerating landing, like a wrist.
retract
200 ms, cubic-bezier(.4,0,.8,.4), the tail chasing the head off the end. Always faster than the draw.
pointer
enter draws, leave retracts; leaving mid-draw retracts from wherever the pen is.
touch
no hover at all. A tap commits the selected mark only — nothing is left sticking after a finger lifts.
keyboard
Tab shows the focus mark (:focus-visible only, never on click). Enter / Space selects.
reduced motion
every mark appears complete, instantly; retract is instant. Same end states, no stroke travel.
never
hover never re-marks what is already marked (current, selected). Focus always shows.
RECOMMENDATION

B · Underline & tick. It is the only grammar that works everywhere the site has state — nav, prose links, filters, a primary action, and keyboard focus — without borrowing space it doesn't have. Each mark has one meaning, the coral is spent once at rest (the current-page scribble, which is literally the identity stroke), and focus is a different shape rather than a different colour.

Keep A's loop and C's arrow as moments, not states: a loop for the one thing a page wants you to notice (a "new" essay), arrows for the home desk's annotations, where they already live. Drop D, or keep a wash only for the Reading page's text selection colour. Watch: the scribble is drawn once per page arrival, never on every hover; the tick needs ~18px of free space before a word, so keep filter gaps ≥ 24px.
