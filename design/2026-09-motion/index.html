FYANG0507.GITHUB.IO · FRONT-END DESIGN AUDIT · 2026-09-27

The drawings are alive. The page isn’t yet.画是活的，页面还没活。

The identity is strong and coherent: a desk whose objects are doors, a bookshelf of essays, a corkboard of projects, polaroids on a line, a specimen card. The drawings already move on a hand’s clock (the book flips, the portrait blinks, the camera goes 咔嚓). What’s missing is everything in between: between pages, between states, between the paper objects and your hand, and between the site and the person visiting it.

method: every page walked at 1440 and 390 · hover / click / flip states captured · source read · judged against fred-visual-design-guide/aesthetic.md

01Four gaps 四个缺口

No continuity没有连续性

Every click is a hard cut followed by the same generic fade-and-rise. The laptop you click disappears instead of arriving, even though every subpage’s tab nav shows that same laptop.

States are colour swaps状态只是换颜色

Hover turns text coral. Filters are SaaS pills. Focus is a browser outline. Your signature mark, the coral hand underline, never responds to anything.

Paper without weight纸没有重量

Pinned cards, hanging polaroids and shelved books are drawn as physical things but scroll like a carousel. Nothing swings, leans or settles.

The site doesn’t notice you网站没注意到你

The bird hops at random. The laptop types a canned line forever, when it could type what Fred actually made last. On a personal site, that noticing is the warmth.

02The motion thesis 会动的手

Two clocks.Drawings move on the hand’s clock: held poses and stepped frames, like Nichijou. Paper and chrome move on the physics clock: mass, momentum, one small overshoot, then settle. A plain 300 ms fade is never the main event.
The pen is the only highlighter.Hover, focus, selected and current are all hand-drawn marks (loop, underline, tick, bracket) in one confident pass. Each mark is seeded to its element, so the same word always gets the same circle.
What you touch is what arrives.Objects keep their identity across states and pages, through shared elements, FLIP and View Transitions.
Every motion has a cause.A pin pivot, wind, photographic development, page weight, a bird noticing you. If a motion has no cause, it’s decoration, and ambient loops without a subject get cut.
One sharp edge.Coral is spent once per view. The quiet around the one moment is what makes it land.
Lean away.Gradients, glossy glare, shimmer sweeps, glass blur, blur-to-focus, parallax for its own sake, cursor trails, scroll-jacking, staggered fade-ups.
