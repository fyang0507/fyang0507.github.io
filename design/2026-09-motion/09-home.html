A desk that is lived at 有人住的桌子

The desk is the site's best idea, but once the opener ends it is a still picture. Objects react only while a hotspot is hovered; the laptop types a canned “$ make life” forever; the bird hops at random whether or not anyone is there; nothing tells a returning visitor what changed; the opener's “loading…” is a fixed 3.4 s whether or not anything is loading; and on a phone the desk shrinks to a strip with half the screen blank under four underlined links.

RECOMMENDATION

Ship D, then B and A together; hold C. D is not optional — the phone home is currently the weakest screen on the site, and the camera walk keeps the desk as one drawing instead of cutting it into cards. B replaces the only untrue thing on the page with real, linked activity, and is the best statement of the thesis (a hand typing, a machine answering). A's bird and portrait make the desk feel occupied without asking for anything; keep them to exactly these two reactions (the steam lean is the most expendable of the three).

C is charming but belongs later and narrower: returning visitors only (no first-visit note — that is an onboarding tooltip in paper), and never when nothing changed and the gap is under a week. If shipped, it should share B's “latest” digest so the note and the screen always agree. Watch that A never interrupts a hop mid-air and that the bird never moves toward the cursor — the moment it follows you it becomes a pet, and that is 媚.

The opener: make it honest, and shorter. Today it is a fixed 3.4 s “loading…” once per session, whether the desk is cached or not. Proposal: (1) play it only on a visitor's first-ever visit (localStorage, not sessionStorage); (2) drop the word “loading…” and let the hopping birds be the load — iris opens when img.decode() on the desk resolves, with a floor of 1.1 s (so the gag lands) and a ceiling of 2.4 s; (3) every later visit gets a 450 ms iris only, and the tag counts real visits — 日常 · ep.07 — which shares C's visit counter and makes the Nichijou joke true; (4) reduced motion or a tap skips it outright, as today.
