The opener 开场

Today’s opener is a black screen, five birds hopping in a row, “日常 · ep.01” and a fixed 3.4 s fake “loading…”, then an iris. It was meant as a Nichijou tribute, but it’s neither of Nichijou’s two registers: the manic OP (hard cuts, loud flat colour, giant type) or the deadpan eyecatch (one ordinary object floating in cream). Nichijou’s comedy lives in the jump between them. Your own guide says register breaks are rare and expensive, and the opener is exactly the one moment that earns one.

shared rules for all four: ≤ 3.2 s first visit · ≤ 0.6 s returning · any click / key / wheel fast-forwards in ~250 ms · the reveal waits for the real image decode, never a fake wait · ≤ 3 flashes/s (WCAG 2.3.1) · reduced motion = no opener · lands pixel-exact on the live desk
