    RECOMMENDATION

    Ship this one. It changes exactly what Fred asked and nothing he accepted. The OP, name beat a, the hard cut and the eyecatch are all round 2’s. The gag between them is gone. It’s shorter too: 3.26 s against 3.68, back under the original brief’s 3.5 s cap.

    The bird. I kept it on the desk from the cut instead of having it hop in last. The OP’s final shot is its close-up and its “…”, so the cut lands on the same bird, small and alone on the empty desk. Then it stands there, unflinching, while the desk crashes down around it. That gets the deadpan out of doing nothing: no entrance and no reaction, so nothing to chain. It also gives the cold-load hold a subject that isn’t a spinner. Hopping in after the plant would cost about 0.4 s and turn the bird back into an event. The portrait is the one that reacts, surprised, and the bird isn’t: that’s 萌 not 媚.

    Watch-outs. The eyecatch (日常 · ep.NN) can be removed. It’s 0.4 s and is also what hides the camera’s return to the page layout; without it, the first visit would end like the returning run, dissolving in the page’s own framing. On a cold connection the wait is real: the mockup’s sprites weigh 2.9 MB, and production derivatives would shorten the hold a lot. The returning hand-off is near-identical, not pixel-identical, so it dissolves over 120 ms instead of cutting.
