BOARD 03

Filters that belong to the shelf 筛选

Writing and Gallery each carry two full rows of mono pill chips (category, then year) plus a “Showing 27 / 27 essays · 显示 27 / 27 篇” line. It is the most generic, SaaS-looking element on the site, and it sits between a beautiful title and the bookshelf eating ~150px. On a phone it wraps into seven rows before any content. And filtering is an instant re-render with no continuity: the books just blink.

What changed 改了什么

The count line was the filter all along; it just never admitted it. Two rows of chips and a status line become one sentence you can edit: Showing all 27 essays from every year / 显示全部 27 篇，所有年份. Only the words that can change are underlined — pencil when they say “everything”, coral once they say something narrower, so the one accent means “this shelf is filtered”.

The sentence wraps to two short lines and the shelf becomes a horizontal strip you thumb through, so spines appear within the first screen instead of after seven rows of chips. The slip clamps to the screen edge; options are 30px+ tall touch targets.

What changed 改了什么

Categories become paper index dividers standing behind the books: their lower half hides behind the shelf's top edge, the chosen one rises clear of it, empty ones sink out of reach. There is no year row at all — the shelf already prints its year ticks, so the ticks are the year filter. Circle one or several; unchosen years collapse to a narrow empty slot that keeps its tick, so the ruler never loses a year you might circle next.

On a phone 手机上

The dividers become one band you can swipe sideways; the ruler rides under the strip of spines. One band plus the shelf instead of seven rows.

Gallery 在拍

Dividers need a thing to stand behind. On the clothesline page they would hang as paper tags clipped to the top line (8 categories), and the date stamps already written on each polaroid would become the year toggles — workable, but it is a second design, not the same component.

The most physical and the most “Fred's shelf”: dividers on springs, years circled in pen on the shelf's own ruler. Also the most machinery, and the year targets are small.

What changed 改了什么

The shelf stands on a ruler of every year, 2026 → 2015, in the same direction as the books. Each essay is one countable dot above its year (2020 honestly has none). Two pen brackets window the years; drag them and books outside the window leave while you are still dragging. Categories are one line of plain words, the chosen one circled.

On a phone 手机上

Words scroll as a single line; the ruler shortens its labels to ’26 … ’15 and stays fixed under the strip, brackets draggable by thumb (touch-action: none on the handles only).

Gallery 在拍

The best fit of the three for photos: 107 frames over 2015–2025 make the dots a real, readable histogram (29 in 2024), and windowing a trip’s years is exactly how you browse a photo archive.

The most dynamic: scrubbing time and watching the shelf empty and refill is the one dense event. But a range slider is still a dashboard idiom, and 27 essays rarely need a window.
cost medium: ruler + two sliders; throttled to one reflow per year crossed, ≤ 1 per 90ms · a11y brackets are role=slider (← → Home End, aria-valuetext); year labels click to isolate that year · support pointer capture, ResizeObserver
RECOMMENDATION

A · Sentence filter. It removes the most (three rows become one sentence, seven phone rows become two lines), it is the only candidate that reads as a person talking rather than a control panel, and it transfers to Gallery by swapping two nouns. The movement lives where it should: in the books, through the shared reflow engine, not in the control.

Borrow one thing from each of the others: C’s countable dots could sit inside the year slip (one dot per essay beside each year), and B’s clickable year ticks are a cheap second door into the same year state if Fred wants it. Ship the engine regardless of which control wins — it is the part that fixes “the books just blink”. Watch the coral budget: the underline turns coral only while a filter is active.
