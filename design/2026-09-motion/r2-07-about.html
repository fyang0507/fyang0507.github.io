FRED, ON ROUND 1

“I like C, that people should drag it out from a case first; then do A so they flip it themselves.”

One card, one sleeve, one continuous gesture. The specimen now arrives tucked in C's kraft sleeve with only its edge showing. You pull it toward you, with friction. At the end its top edge catches on the lip: the lip sags, the sleeve comes along for a beat, then lets go with a slap and two frames of impact ticks. The card lifts into your hand at full size and becomes A: tilt with real thickness, throw to flip, radar drawn on arrival at night.

The sleeve stays on the desk behind the card as the place it goes back to. Push the card back up (or tap the sleeve) and it slides back in. C's two-card swap and B's peel are retired, and the glare, shimmer and coral glow stay gone.

RECOMMENDATION

Ship this as the About card. It keeps what Fred liked about each round-1 direction and adds none of their costs. From C it keeps the ritual of taking the specimen out, but not the two-card swap, so day and night stay two sides of one person. From A it keeps holding and throwing, but that now comes second, so the first touch is something you earn. The WOW is the pull itself: the name clears the lip last, and the top edge catches for a beat before the sleeve lets go.

Watch: keep the catch short (≈170 ms held; longer reads as a bug). Keep the impact ticks to two frames and never repeat them on the re-sleeve. The page grows as the card comes up to full size. That is intentional, since the card needs its room, but the live page should reserve the in-hand height below the fold if layout shift matters there. The desk scale (about half size) makes the card's small print texture while it is still in the sleeve, and only the name reads. That is deliberate.

执行模式：精修（第二轮 · 延续 A 与 C，合并成一个连续动作） 表达锚点：标本卡装在牛皮纸卡套里，口朝向你。先把它抽出来，再拿在手里、甩一下、翻过来。 动作时钟：物理时钟 —— 抽出（先粘住、再打滑、带摩擦）、卡在卡套口（卡套口下垂、卡套被带着走）、松脱（卡套弹回并扭一下）、拿起到手里（放大、前倾、一次小回弹）、A 的倾斜 / 甩动翻面 / 半色调接触阴影、推回卡套；手的时钟 —— 松脱瞬间两帧冲击线（不插值）、小鸟的定格姿势、雷达逐笔画出、卡套清单上的钢笔勾。 映射：手指往下拖 → 卡片从卡套里滑出，摩擦让它比手指慢；卡片上沿碰到卡套口 → 卡住、卡套口被拉弯、卡套被拖动；拉力超过摩擦 → 卡片弹出，卡套弹回并因一角先松而扭转；卡片离开桌面 → 放大到手里的尺寸，接触阴影出现；第一次拿到某一面 → 卡套清单打勾；抵达夜面 → 见证者开始记录；往上推 → 放回桌面、推进卡套。 线条计划：卡套边缘、缝线、纤维、冲击线、提示箭头都是单笔、钝头、铅笔 / 墨色；悬停只用笔的下划线。 色彩计划：牛皮纸为结构色；日面珊瑚色只留给手写批注，夜面珊瑚色只留给雷达多边形；无高光、无流光、无光晕，接触阴影用网点。
