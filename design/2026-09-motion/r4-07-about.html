Round 3 gave the specimen card its shape (7:12) but kept round 2's direction: the sleeve stood up and the card came out of its bottom, toward you. For a long card that is the less likely way to store it. Card sleeves, tarot boxes and library pockets open on a short end, and you slide the card out lengthwise. Fred asked for exactly that: out of the right side.

“Instead of taking out from the bottom, can we take it out from the side (right side)? More realistic design with a long card.”

RECOMMENDATION

Ship A along the long axis. This is the realistic version of Fred's note. A long card is stored in a sleeve that fits it end-on, so it comes out lengthwise: the pull is long (1.6 card widths), the name clears the lip last, and the quarter turn into your hand is a real beat, not decoration, because that is how you'd pick up a card that was lying on its side. It also fits better. With the sleeve beside the card instead of above it, the upright card gets the screen's whole height.

The short-axis alternative (the toggle under the stage) keeps the sleeve standing, with its mouth on the long right edge, and the card slides out sideways with no turn. It is cheaper and the pull is easy to fit on a phone, but the travel is half as long (0.9 card widths) and the catch has less to hold. A sleeve open along a long edge is also the one that lets a card fall out. It reads as a wallet slot, not a keepsake sleeve.

Watch: in the hand the card now shares its axis between two gestures. A drag to the right throws it over, a drag to the left (toward the sleeve) puts it back, and a throw to the left no longer flips it. A tap and Enter still flip. On a phone the sleeve gives about a sixth of its length (25px) past the left edge while you pull, which is what keeps the finger inside the screen, then springs back. The re-sleeve's first beat is autonomous: the card is set down on its side by the mouth (on a phone that is to the right of where it was held), and only then does the drag push it in.

执行模式：精修（第四轮 · 改抽出方向，卡与卡面不变） 表达锚点：长卡平放在卡套里，从右侧开口顺着长边抽出来，拿起来时再竖过来；像从书签袋、塔罗牌盒里抽出一张卡。 动作时钟：物理时钟 —— 往右抽（先粘住、再打滑；摩擦随套里剩下的长度递减；握卡套的那只手往反方向让一点）、卡在卡套口（卡套口向右弯）、松脱（两只手各自一顿，卡套弹回并扭一下）、拿起（放大、前倾），稍后四分之一转竖起来（一次小回弹）、倾斜、往右甩翻面、半色调接触阴影；放回：往左拖 → 先放下并转回横向，再推进卡套；手的时钟 —— 两帧冲击线、小鸟的定格姿势、雷达逐笔画出、卡套清单上的钢笔勾。 映射：手指往右拖 → 卡片从右侧滑出，套里剩得越多越慢；卡片末端碰到卡套口 → 卡住、卡套口被拉弯；拉力超过摩擦 → 卡片弹出；拿起 → 放大并竖起来，卡套整个露在左边；往右拖 → 翻面；往左拖（朝卡套口）→ 放下、转横、推进去；竖向滑动一律交给页面滚动。 版式：桌面 —— 卡套在中线左边，卡在它右边同一条中线上，拿在手里的卡 432×740，整段动作一屏之内、不推动页面；手机 —— 卡套在左上，卡往右抽（卡套往左让出最多四分之一），竖起来落到中间。 笔：悬停下划线与焦点「 」用珊瑚色（短暂）；选中用麦色色带；提示箭头用 r3-02 的箭头字形（短、近直、落点端正），珊瑚色，是本视图唯一的“指”，第一次抽出后本次会话不再出现。 色彩计划：牛皮纸为结构色；静止时珊瑚色只在日面手写批注 / 夜面雷达（以及抽出前的一支提示箭头）；无高光、无流光、无光晕，接触阴影用网点。
