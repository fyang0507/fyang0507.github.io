01 · the common pattern

The agent handles the seams. The human keeps the decision.

The useful automation was not a replacement for Joe's judgment. It was a way to move repetitive assembly, validation, and verification out of his way.

  1. 01 · inputFragmented systems

    Files, vendors, exports, links, constraints.

  2. 02 · agentAssemble + verify

    Normalize, generate, validate, check.

  3. 03 · handoffHuman judgment

    Approve the whole result and decide.

  4. 04 · outcomeOne experience

    A public listing or a reviewed homeowner message.

automation does not remove the handoff; it makes the handoff worth having
