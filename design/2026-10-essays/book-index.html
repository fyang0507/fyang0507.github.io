Play

The board's two pages are the real Writing and Reading pages, run from design/ with the real shelf, essays, header and fonts; only the move between them is the board's (book-vt.js). Pick a candidate, pull a book off the shelf (hover; on a phone tap a spine), then click it. Back (⌘[) is the way back, with the book still in your hand; “← 全部文章” loads a fresh shelf. The switcher in the lower left changes the candidate and plays the move at a quarter speed (×¼). Resize the window to 390 px for the phone.

Three candidates

A 扉页铺开the title page opens outRECOMMENDED

扉页的纸铺开成整页，书名从扉页飞到文章标题的位置：书上读到的，就是页上读到的。保留现在的翻开动作，只把最后的硬切换成连续；这正是 2026-09 那轮书架板子上“纸从扉页长成阅读页、标题飞过去”的设想，第一次真的跨页实现。

The paper of the title page grows from the book to the window on a spring, the print on it goes, and the title flies to where the essay prints it. Once the sheet fills the window, the essay prints through it.

way back
The essay unprints to paper, the sheet folds back onto the title page and the title flies back down onto it; then the book goes back on the shelf.
costs
On a phone, a title that wraps differently on the narrow title page and in the essay re-sets itself mid-flight (a short crossfade).

B 封面成题图the cover becomes the plate

取下腰封，封面的照片展开成文章顶部的题图：手里那本书的封面，就是文章的头图。省掉“翻开”那一下，书架早 0.25 秒交出页面。

The book comes square to you and the obi slips off; the board's photo opens out into the essay's plate: its frame grows from the board to the band at the top of the window while the photo inside stays the same photo, at one scale.

way back
The plate folds back into the cover, and the obi slides back on.
costs
Changes the open gesture: the board no longer swings open to the title page. The essay's title and nav appear only as the plate lands. Needs the cover fetched while the book opens, and its aspect.

C 翻过扉页turn the title page

像读书一样翻过扉页：扉页绕书脊翻过来，它的背面就是文章，翻到底时铺满整个窗口。沿用书页翻动的语言（Writing 的 tab 动作也是翻页）。

The title page lifts and turns over on the spine toward you; its other side is the essay, which comes round to fill the window.

way back
The essay turns back over onto the title page.
costs
The loudest of the three: a full-window 3-D turn. The essay is seen in perspective while it may still be mounting.

The same in all three

  • The header strip rides from Writing's place to Reading's on the tab moves' spring; the identity lifts away with Writing.
  • Every animation is transform or opacity on the pseudo-elements; a clip (B's frame) is set but holds still. Chrome's own trace composites every one, both ways, at 1440 and 390.
  • Reduced motion: no transition (the inline opt-in), the hard cut. Back through the back/forward cache: the move in reverse onto the book in your hand, then the book goes back. The “all writing” link (a fresh shelf, not drawn yet when the page arrives): the tab moves' paper swap.
  • Safari (WebKit 26.6) plays all three as Chrome does.
  • From the click to the essay at rest (measured, 1440): today 1.0 s (the open gesture, then the cut); A 1.6 s, B 1.5 s, C 1.7 s. The essay mounts underneath while the move plays.
