A · 书架 · the shelfA short stretch of Writing's shelf. Line-led, the same books as on Writing.

文末接上 Writing 的书架：这一本在你手里，架上留个空位，左右两本向空位歪着，书名写在旁边，伸手就能抽下一本。没有下一篇时站着书架上那个虚线的「つづく」，没有上一篇时书架就到头了：少一个邻居只是少一本书，不会有卡片撑满整行。

The book you are reading is out, so its slot is empty and its neighbours lean into the gap, as Writing's do when you pull one. Each neighbour is the same book as on the shelf (width, height and tone come from its place in the order), named on a label beside its spine. A printed slip on the plank's lip goes back to the shelf.

  • One neighbour: Writing's dashed つづく ghost stands where a newer essay would (“下一篇 · 还在写”); before the oldest the shelf simply ends (“这是最早的一篇”). The plank is the same length in every case.
  • Hover: the book comes up out of its lean on the physics clock, leaving its contact stipple behind, and the pen underlines its name. Keyboard: the coral 「 」. Reduced motion: nothing moves, the pen still marks it.
  • Phone: each name stands over its book, so the gap stays between the books. About 190 px tall on the desk, 250 on a phone. No images.

Reading 左边那条铅笔线读完打了 ✓ 之后接着往下走，串起三行目录：上一篇在上、这一篇（麦色高亮）居中、下一篇在下，「上」「下」正好就是字面上的上下。没有卡片，只有字、一条铅笔线和笔的状态；没有下一篇时线变成虚线，走向「つづく」。

Three ruled lines in the order the essays were written: date, title, length and tag, and on the right what each one is. The graphite line runs down the margin at the rail's own x (56 px left of the text) and ticks each line; this essay carries the wheat band, the current mark.

recommendation

A, the shelf. It is the most this site's own: the end of an essay becomes the shelf the essay came from, so going on reads as putting this book back and taking the next. It answers both halves of the note at once: it looks like Writing, not a template, and a missing neighbour is one book fewer, with the site's own つづく doing the explaining. It is compact, costs no image bytes, and a book looks the same here as on the shelf. B is the more inviting (real covers pull you on) but twice as tall and two images heavier; C is the quietest and the safest, the one to take if the end of an essay should stay purely typographic.

Writing's own shelf runs the other way, newest first with つづく at its left, so A's little shelf is its mirror. Mirror A to match Writing (下一篇 would then stand on the left), or keep left = older?
