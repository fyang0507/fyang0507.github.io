The seals, re-carved · 重刻白文

Fred picked B 印, with one change: “let 弗 be on one col and 雷德 on the other.” Read the seal the way seals are read, right column first, top to bottom: 弗 alone fills the right column, 雷 over 德 the left. Everything else in B stands: FRED YANG, the written motto, the pen’s states, the arrival, the accessible name.

This lockup is also what production now carries: the markup in every static header is baked from r2-seal.js.

The re-carve

Drawn large, beside round 1’s carving, then at 1:1.

  • Columns: 雷德 share the dense column, cut wider (51 units of 100); 弗, five strokes, takes the narrow one (31) and runs the full height, its 撇 and 竖 continuing under the 弓.
  • Knife: 疏密, the sparse column cut a knife wider (4.5 units against 3.2), so its four verticals keep about as much red between them as 雷德’s densest strokes do (about 4 units).
  • Border: one margin all round, about 3.5 units of red to the stone’s edge, and the same red between the columns. The stone keeps round 1’s hand-cut edge and its one nick; each impression keeps its tilt.
  • At 66 px: 雷 and 德 hold at 1×; 弗’s narrow column reads fully at 2×, and at 1× as a seal’s texture. The accessible name carries the text either way.

Recommendation: the seal. Every other header now carries it, so an essay is stamped with the same mark as the page it came from, in both themes (dark mode’s coral and paper recolour it). The portrait is a 512 px drawing shrunk to 34 px, the rescale the visual guide warns about, and it stays where it belongs, as the favicon. At this size the seal reads as the mark rather than as characters; its name is “Fred Yang”, as the portrait’s was. Ported in its own commit, so it can be dropped alone.

b · The status line beside the identity on phones

The header row already wraps only when it must. The seals are narrow enough that the status fits beside them from 344 px on Writing and About (328 on Building, 352 on Gallery), so on every common phone from 360 up; at 320 it wraps, as today.

Recommendation: let it sit beside wherever it fits. It gives a phone header back a line (about 35 px) without a new rule, and nothing is hidden or shrunk: the status keeps its 10.5 px, and at 320 it wraps under the identity as before.
