01Featured demo · one task across several surfaces

Meet Me Wherever I Am

Concept preview

What happens

The agent calls me. I send a YouTube link by message. It understands the video, asks for a tapback, and writes the approved artifact into my technical-reading system.

No single interface contains the interaction. The task survives anyway.

Recipe

general-purpose agent→ voice call→ message handoff→ video understanding→ human approval→ Notion record

What to notice

  • The live call stays fast and bounded.
  • The task can wait across channels without losing context.
  • Approval is lightweight, explicit, and followed by durable state.

Current limitation

A bounded composition, not a universal cross-channel engine.

Walkthrough, caller-ID capture, and final Notion record still to add.

02Demo · Trash Patrol

The House Has a Small, Boring Problem

Concept preview

What happens

Sundial wakes the workflow near sunset. A doorbell frame, the local collection rule, and a bounded judgment answer one question: do the bins need attention now?

The system posts a short verdict and deletes the temporary capture.

Recipe

solar trigger→ doorbell capture→ local rule and visual judgment→ short Discord verdict→ cleanup

What to notice

  • The trigger follows the obligation, not a generic schedule.
  • Unclear scenes produce uncertainty, not a guess.
  • The final message is smaller than the reasoning behind it.

Current limitation

A bounded household check, not general visual understanding of the home.

Public evidence

Sanitized frame, schedule receipt, and verdict still to add.

03Demo · Discord Intake

Drop the Messy Input

Concept preview

What happens

I drop unfinished text or media into one Discord channel. The workflow stages attachments, understands what matters, performs the requested action, and advances its cursor only after the result is safe.

The human interface is deliberately undisciplined. The discipline lives behind it.

Recipe

phone capture→ Discord staging→ media understanding→ requested action→ local artifact or Notion→ cursor advance

What to notice

  • Capture does not require choosing the final destination.
  • Temporary attachments are staged before they expire.
  • A failed write does not silently consume the message.

Current limitation

The workflow may organize ambiguity; it should not invent missing intent.

Public evidence

Public intake, durable result, and retry example still to add.

04Demo · Unattended Recovery

Failure Should Leave a Forwarding Address

Concept preview

What happens

The workflow preserves retry state, saves a local evidence note, and posts a concise digest. If diagnosis is needed, a separate investigator works under a recursion guard and returns with a final status.

The human learns what did not happen and what comes next—not merely that a log exists.

Recipe

failure→ preserve retry state→ save evidence→ concise human digest→ guarded investigation→ final status

What to notice

  • Failed side effects do not advance completion state.
  • Routine no-ops and clear rate limits avoid needless investigation.
  • The final message closes the human-visible loop.

Current limitation

It makes failure legible; it cannot make every external surface reliable.

Public evidence

Failure digest, preserved-state timeline, and final status still to add.

05Supporting demo · Co-authored Publishing

Continuity Is More Than a Prompt

Concept preview

What happens

The Article and Visual Design Guides preserve source, judgment, revision, and adaptable boundaries across sessions.

The point is not imitation on command. A future agent inherits the editorial world around the artifact.

Recipe

source material→ canonical base draft→ article guide→ human revision→ visual direction→ destination-specific build→ verified publication

What to notice

  • Source remains distinct from platform adaptations.
  • Human decisions are preserved rather than averaged away.
  • Publication includes real rendering and verification.

Current limitation

Continuity reduces re-explanation; it does not replace authorship.

Public evidence

Source-to-revision sequence and verified result still to add.
